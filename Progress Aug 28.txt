2. We investigated the treatment-response features instead of blindly trusting them

This was probably the most important technical work today.

We established that antibiotic information is stored hourly as:

active_antibiotic_count

and verified that:

$$ \text{antibiotic intensification}_t=1 $$

when:

$$ A_t>A_{t-1} $$

where \(A_t\) is the number of active antibiotics.

Our independent calculation produced exactly:

55,879 antibiotic-intensification hours.

We also confirmed:

Antibiotic-treatment hours = 1,645,913
ICU stays with antibiotic intensification = 24,037
Maximum concurrent antibiotics = 7

Then we investigated delta_dose.

Using the processed vasopressor dose from Variant B:

$$ \Delta Dose_t=Dose_t-Dose_{t-1} $$

we independently reproduced:

272,507 non-zero ΔDose hours

with:

0 mismatching ΔDose rows.

That gave us strong evidence that Variant C's dynamic treatment features were actually constructed consistently.
